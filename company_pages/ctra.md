The Merger is subject to a number of conditions to the closing, as specified in the Merger Agreement. We can provide no assurance that the required stockholder approvals will be obtained or that the required conditions to the closing will be satisfied. These conditions to the completion of the Merger, some of which are beyond our control, may not be satisfied or waived in a timely manner or at all, and, accordingly, the Merger may be delayed or may not be completed at all.
